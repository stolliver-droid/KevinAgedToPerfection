Distillery Prep

Buffalo Trace Distillery

Frankfort, KY · Friday Shop Stop · 9:00 AM

America's oldest continuously operating distillery, founded in 1773. No tour on this visit — we're swinging through the gift shop to stock up on bottles that are hard to find back home.

Founded 1773 Frankfort, KY Shop opens 9 AM Free parking

What to look for: Blanton's Single Barrel, Eagle Rare 10yr, W.L. Weller, E.H. Taylor Jr., and Buffalo Trace Antique Collection bottles. Stock rotates daily — check availability before you go:

📋 Check Today's Product Availability →

Castle & Key Distillery

Frankfort, KY · Friday Tour · 3:15 PM

Built in 1887 as the Old Taylor Distillery, Castle & Key is one of Kentucky's most stunning properties — a fully restored historic estate with formal gardens, a castle-like springhouse, and a limestone quarry pond.

Built 1887 Restored 2018 Tour at 3:15 PM Arrive by 2:45

What to expect: A guided tour through the historic grounds and production facility, followed by a tasting. Known for their Restoration Rye, Sacred Spring Botanical Gin, and small-batch bourbon. The property alone is worth the visit.
